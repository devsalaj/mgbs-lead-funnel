The MGBS Executive Framework

The "3 Ds" Diagnostic Engine

Every enterprise friction point stems from three core vulnerabilities: Defects, Deviations, or Delays. Select an area below to inspect the symptoms, root causes, and customized Six Sigma remedies.

⚠️ Pillar 1: Defects (Quality & Yield)
Primary Focus: Scrap Reduction & First-Time-Right (FTR)
🚨

Operational Symptoms

  • High scrap and off-spec finished goods generation
  • Repeated rework loops eating into operating margin
  • Customer defect escapes triggering warranty liabilities
  • Process Capability index Cpk persistently < 1.33
🔍

Root Cause Analysis

  • Unidentified Special Cause statistical variation
  • Measurement System Analysis (MSA) & Gage R&R failures
  • Inadequate tolerance stack-up and raw material drift
  • Hidden factory operations masking true yield loss
💡

MGBS Six Sigma Solution

  • DMAIC Quality Breakthrough: Targeted project chartering
  • Design of Experiments (DOE): Parameter optimization
  • Mistake-Proofing (Poka-Yoke): Physical process interlocks
  • Failure Mode & Effects Analysis: Risk mitigation
"Eliminating defects at the source delivers immediate cash-flow relief and restores customer trust."
📊 Pillar 2: Deviations (Process Stability)
Primary Focus: Standard Work & Statistical Process Control
🚨

Operational Symptoms

  • Significant batch-to-batch and shift-to-shift performance variance
  • Non-conformance reports (NCRs) and recurring audit findings
  • "Tribal knowledge" dependency; each operator runs equipment differently
  • Frequent emergency parameter tweaking and unapproved recipe adjustments
🔍

Root Cause Analysis

  • Absence of live Statistical Process Control (SPC) charting
  • Unstandardized Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)
  • Insufficient human factors engineering in workstation layout
  • Disconnect between design specifications and manufacturing capability
💡

MGBS Six Sigma Solution

  • Statistical Process Control (SPC): Real-time control limits
  • The 8 Rs of Sustenance Architecture: Habitual governance
  • Standard Work Standardization: Visual shop-floor job aids
  • Layered Process Auditing (LPA): Accountability feedback
"Stability precedes capability. You cannot optimize a process that is not under statistical control."
⏱️ Pillar 3: Delays (Throughput & Velocity)
Primary Focus: Cycle Time Reduction & WIP Inventory Unblocking
🚨

Operational Symptoms

  • Chronic delivery SLA misses and extended customer lead times
  • Excessive Work-in-Progress (WIP) clogging factory floors
  • Bottlenecks shifting unpredictably across production cells
  • Frequent changeover downtime and high batch setup penalties
🔍

Root Cause Analysis

  • Muda (Waste) accumulation in non-value-added queue times
  • Unbalanced line takt times and capacity mismatches
  • Push-system scheduling disconnected from actual demand pull
  • Lengthy equipment changeovers without SMED methodology
💡

MGBS Six Sigma Solution

  • Value Stream Mapping (VSM): End-to-end flow visibility
  • Single-Minute Exchange of Die (SMED): Fast changeovers
  • Pull / Kanban Architecture: Slashing WIP buffer traps
  • Kaizen Burst Events: Rapid 5-day cycle time compression
"Velocity in operations is the ultimate multiplier of enterprise profitability and capital turnover."
